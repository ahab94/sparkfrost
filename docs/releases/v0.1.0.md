**Play in your browser, on a computer or phone:** https://ahab94.github.io/sparkfrost/

The first release of **Sparkfrost**: two princess sisters, one of ice and one of fire, solve snowy puzzles together.

### What's in it
- **15 levels** that teach one idea at a time and build up to teamwork puzzles. Every level is proven beatable by a built-in solver, and par is the fewest moves possible.
- **Frost** freezes water into ice bridges. **Spark** melts ice and lights the lanterns that open gates. Push ice blocks into water to make bridges.
- **A little story** about the sisters on their way to light the Northern Lights again, with expressions and emotes that react to what happens.
- **Original music and sounds:** a music-box waltz and sparkly effects, all synthesized from scratch.
- **Phone controls:** on-screen pads for each sister, upright or sideways.

### Controls
| | Frost ❄️ | Spark 🔥 |
|---|---|---|
| Keyboard | WASD to move, Space for power | Arrows to move, Enter for power |
| Phone | Left pad, middle button for power | Right pad, middle button for power |

Z undo · R restart · M music
